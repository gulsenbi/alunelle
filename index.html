Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2026

Summary: We do not run servers that store your cycle data. Everything you log in Alunelle lives on your device, and — if you enable iCloud sync — in your personal Apple iCloud account.

  • No account & no tracking: Alunelle never asks for your name, email, or phone number, and embeds zero advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • On-device encryption: Free-text notes are encrypted on device with AES-GCM using a 256-bit key stored in your iOS Keychain.
  • Apple Health (optional): If you grant permission, Alunelle reads/writes menstrual flow and basal body temperature directly with Apple HealthKit on your device.
  • Delete anytime: Delete all local and iCloud data anytime from Settings → Data & Privacy → Delete all data.

Terms of Use & Medical Disclaimer

Not medical advice or contraception: Alunelle is a personal cycle and wellness journal. Its predictions are statistical estimates and must not be used as a contraceptive method or to diagnose, treat, or prevent any medical condition.

Alunelle Plus Subscriptions: Payment is charged to your Apple ID account. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. Standard Apple EULA applies: Apple Standard EULA.
